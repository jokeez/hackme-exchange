What HMC is for

HMC pays for security testing. Teams order Dig and Hunt fuzzing campaigns on the HackMe network and pay in HMC; the network's machines search the software for real bugs and are paid from those orders and from block rewards.

  • From block 550,000 (Economics v2) each block rewards 0.1 HMC, and 5% of every fuzzing order is burned.
  • If you order fuzzing, buy HMC here and pay from your HackMe wallet — the HackMe Orders profile works without mining.

Buy in five steps

  1. Get USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). Buy it on any exchange or wallet and withdraw on the BSC / BEP-20 network — not TRON, not Ethereum. Coins sent on another network cannot be credited.
  2. Open an exchange account. On exchange.hackme.tech go to Account → Connect. Your account key is created in this browser: download the recovery file when asked and keep it offline — without it, clearing the browser loses access to your balance.
  3. Deposit USDT. Account → Deposit → USDT shows your personal BSC address. Send USDT there. After 15 BSC confirmations (about a minute) the deposit is screened for sanctions/stolen funds, then becomes available.
  4. Buy HMC. Convert swaps instantly at the market price — good for small amounts. For larger amounts use Spot with a limit order so you control the price.
  5. Keep it or withdraw. You can keep HMC on the exchange or withdraw it to your own HackMe wallet address (HackMe app). Withdrawals need two-factor authentication.

Know the risks

  • Soft launch. The exchange is new and still being hardened. Start with amounts you can afford to lose.
  • Custodial. Deposited coins are held in exchange wallets. Check the proof of reserves any time.
  • Thin market. Most liquidity comes from the exchange's own market maker. Prices can move sharply and large orders get worse prices — use limit orders.
  • Price risk. HMC is a young coin; its price can fall a lot or to zero. Nothing here is investment advice.
  • No card or bank payments. Only crypto deposits (USDT BEP-20, HMC, SUP).
